- **Red is kept for the heaviest days.** A day over its budget is orange, and turns red only at twice the budget or more. Days with no budget, such as those before a taper began, are purple, darker the more was drunk, rather than judged against the weekly guideline, so a log that predates its plan no longer reads as a wall of red. With Differentiate Without Colour on, `!!` now marks twice the budget.
